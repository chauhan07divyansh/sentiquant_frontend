# Product

## Register

brand (marketing pages: homepage, about, pricing, blogs)
product (app pages: stocks, portfolio, dashboard)

## Users

Indian retail investors — people who follow the markets, understand what a stock is, and want professional-grade technical and fundamental analysis without needing a Bloomberg terminal or a finance degree. They invest directly in NSE/BSE stocks and are looking for an edge that is both credible and accessible. Context of use: at a desk or on mobile, doing their own research before placing a trade or reviewing a portfolio.

## Product Purpose

Sentiquant analyses Indian stocks using AI — technicals, fundamentals, news sentiment, and MDA — and delivers an actionable score, entry zone, support, and resistance targets in under 60 seconds. The product exists because professional-grade analysis was locked behind expensive tools or required finance expertise to interpret. Success looks like: a user searches a stock, reads the output, and feels genuinely equipped to make a decision — not overwhelmed, not patronised.

## Brand Personality

Clean · Editorial · Intelligent. The emotional goal is quiet confidence: the interface should feel like the best financial journalist in the room, not the loudest broker.

## Anti-references

- Zerodha / Groww / Kuvera — the typical Indian fintech look: colorful, feature-dense, deal-hunt energy, notification badges on everything.
- Robinhood / crypto apps — gamified, neon-lit, pump energy, dopamine-optimized interaction.
- Generic AI SaaS landing pages — cream/sand backgrounds, gradient text, identical card grids, hero metrics with big numbers and small labels, eyebrow on every section.

## Design Principles

1. **Quiet authority** — the interface communicates expertise through restraint, not decoration. If it needs to explain itself, simplify it.
2. **Legibility is non-negotiable** — financial data must be scannable in under two seconds per metric. Color, contrast, and hierarchy exist to serve comprehension first.
3. **Earn the next scroll** — each section of the marketing page should introduce a new idea, not repeat the last one in a different wrapper.
4. **Plain English, no jargon** — every label, signal, and piece of copy should be readable by someone who is not a professional analyst. The AI does the translation; the UI should not require a further translation.
5. **Dark surfaces, zero compromise** — the single dark theme is a brand commitment. Contrast ratios apply regardless. Gray-on-black is not "subtle," it's inaccessible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as the baseline (4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text). The product serves investors across all age groups, including older users who may need higher contrast. Reduced motion must be respected — financial data animated in is better than financial data that never arrives.
